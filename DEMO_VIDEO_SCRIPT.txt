QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION
Bacopa extract 150 mg per daily dose; phosphatidylcholine 2% w/v; glycerol 5% w/v; xylitol 3% w/v.

STANDARDIZATION / FINGERPRINT
50% total bacosides with bacoside A as the primary HPLC marker.

EXTRACTION RATIO AND SOLVENT
Bacopa aerial-part extract, DER 8:1, using 60% hydroethanolic extraction.

DOSE, FREQUENCY AND DURATION
Two metered sprays twice daily for up to 12 weeks.

RELEASE / PERFORMANCE PROFILE
Target at least 80% active release within 20 minutes.

CRITICAL PROCESS PARAMETERS
pH 5.5–6.5; temperature below 35°C; spray volume 0.15 mL ±10%.

EXACT PROPOSED CLAIM
Supports memory, attention and cognitive performance in healthy adults when used daily.

BIOLOGICAL-RESOURCE SOURCING
Cultivated Bacopa sourced through a registered supplier in Kerala, India, with supplier, batch and collection records.

MANUFACTURING / TECHNICAL PROCESS
Incorporate the standardized Bacopa extract into a phospholipid dispersion under controlled shear and pH, then filter and fill into calibrated metered-dose spray containers.

BRAND / PRODUCT NAME
BrahmiQ
